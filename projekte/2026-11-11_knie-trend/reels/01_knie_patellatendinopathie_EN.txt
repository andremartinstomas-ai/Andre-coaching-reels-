# Front of my knee hurts: patellar tendinopathy (trend format, EN)  (14.7 s)

## Caption (zum Posten)
Pain right below your kneecap, especially on stairs, jumping or squats? That often fits patellar tendinopathy. This is not a remote diagnosis though, get your knee checked.

4 exercises you can try, from easier to harder. Start with the hip-dominant step-down, the knee-dominant one is the harder version.

If something hurts, don't go deeper into the movement.

Save this reel for your next knee workout.

#kneepain #patellartendinopathy #jumpersknee #physiotherapy #kneerehab

## Skript
- Hook: Patient holds his knee: 'The front of my knee hurts!!!'
- Aufbau: Physio: 'Where?' → patient points below the kneecap → physio recognises the typical picture: patellar tendinopathy (chef's kiss).
- Payoff: 4 exercises as a progression: Spanish squat → hip-dominant step-down (easier) → knee-dominant step-down (harder) → single-leg squat to box.

## Aussagen & Quellen
