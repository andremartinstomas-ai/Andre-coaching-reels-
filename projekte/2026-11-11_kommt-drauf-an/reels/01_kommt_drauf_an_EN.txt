# It depends: Which exercise for low back pain? (EN)  (14.1 s)

## Caption (zum Posten)
To all physios: how often do you hear this question?

And to everyone with back pain: there is no one exercise that works for everyone. Once it's clear what's behind your pain, the exercise actually fits.

Save this reel and send it to someone who always asks exactly this.

#backpain #physiotherapy #physiohumor #itdepends #painscience

## Skript
- Hook: Patient walks in, holds his back: 'My lower back hurts. Which exercise should I do?'
- Aufbau: Physio thinks, the questions pop up around him (red flags, radiating pain, stress, sleep, goals ...).
- Payoff: Punchline: 'It depends…' There is no one exercise for everyone, it takes a proper assessment.

## Aussagen & Quellen
- "Before choosing exercises for low back pain, warning signs (red flags) and psychosocial factors such as stress or fear of movement should be assessed." -> Bundesärztekammer (BÄK), KBV, AWMF. Nationale VersorgungsLeitlinie Nicht-spezifischer Kreuzschmerz, 2. Auflage 2017 (Kapitel Diagnostik: Warnhinweise und psychosoziale Risikofaktoren/Yellow Flags) [konsens]
