# Outside of my elbow hurts: lateral elbow tendinopathy (EN)  (14.7 s)

## Caption (zum Posten)
Pain on the outside of your elbow, especially when gripping, carrying or typing? That often fits lateral elbow tendinopathy (formerly 'tennis elbow'). This is not a remote diagnosis though, get your elbow checked.

3 exercises you can try, from easier to harder.

If something hurts, don't go deeper into the movement.

Save this reel for your next workout.

## Skript
- Hook: Patient shakes his arm: 'The outside of my elbow hurts!!!'
- Aufbau: Physio: 'Where?' → patient points exactly to the outside of the elbow → physio: lateral elbow tendinopathy (scream).
- Payoff: 3 exercises: isometric hold → pronation & supination with dumbbell → side-lying external rotation, eccentric back to neutral.

## Aussagen & Quellen
- "Pain on the outside of the elbow often fits lateral elbow tendinopathy, the currently accepted term instead of 'lateral epicondylitis' or 'tennis elbow'." -> Bateman M et al. Development of a core outcome set for lateral elbow tendinopathy (COS-LET) using best available evidence and an international consensus process. Br J Sports Med 2022;56(12):657–666 [konsens]
- "Active strengthening of the arm is first-line treatment for lateral elbow tendinopathy and better than wait-and-see." -> Karanasios S, Korakakis V, Moutzouri M et al. Exercise interventions in lateral elbow tendinopathy have better outcomes than passive interventions, but the effects are small: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 2123 subjects in 30 trials. Br J Sports Med 2021;55(9):477–485 [belegt]
